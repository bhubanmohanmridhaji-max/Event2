Rates and charges

The starting rate per plate of each session (one dish in every section is included), and the general charges. Leave a box as it is to keep the value.

Section rules

For each section: how many dishes the rate includes, and the price of every extra dish (per plate).

Edit the menu

For every dish: its extra charge, its dispatch quantity per portion, or hide it from the menu. Hidden dishes can be shown again here.

Add a section

A new section joins the end of that session's menu. Add its dishes with Add a menu item below (choose the new section there).

Add a menu item

The new item joins that section and follows its rate plan automatically (dishes included, and the price of each extra dish). Its dispatch quantity is worked out from the guests and the buffer. Lunch and Dinner share one menu, and so do AMT and PMT.

Special invoice (sweets)

Items for the Spl Invoice page (for example Diwali sweets). Set the rate, whether it is priced per kg or per piece, and the quantities the customer can pick (in kg, for example 0.4, 1, 2, 5).
